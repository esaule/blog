I think I want to stand books behind me when I on camera. So I need bookstands to prop up books. Some of the books are kinda beefy... So need to scale accordingly.

The plan is simple. 

1. Backing board is plywood
2. The leg is fixed, not adjustable. A piece of plywood cut at an angle.
3. The book need to rest on something, another piece of plywood.

Let's go!
