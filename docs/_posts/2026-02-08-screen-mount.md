So I have this not so stable desk in my home office that holds my computer. Thoughthe screens are at the wrong height. They take surface space from the desk. And they make everything kinda heavy to move arond. 

It's time to wall mount the screens.

The plan is simple:

1. French cleat on the wall. This will enable me to easily adjust position of the screen. Later I could mount other things on the wall. (thinking about mic mounts for instance) 
2. Panel on the french cleat that is low enough to have the center of gravity not getting the screens of the cleat
3. Dark stain to match the desk
4. Use a commercial monitor arms

That was EASY! Turns out when you built a dozen stuff like that before, doing two that look decent isn't too hard.

If you are wondering the commercial monitor arms is the one from [suptek](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HJ5BFS52?th=1). The gas hydrolic(whatever it is called) system is NO JOKE! Real the instruction or take a chance to bust your wrist!

But once put together, it really helps. Ok it is still a mess! But not as much!
