There is an office closest with a ton of stuff in it. It's technically a dresser so there are clothes in it. But also box and equipement and documents on the floor. 

The plan is simple. The clothes stay there. the stuff on the floor go in rolling shelves.

1. wide boxes made of plywood with a middle shelf.
2. Glue it all for stability, so middle shelf goes in with a dado.
3. paint it
4. wheel at the bottom


![Boxes]({{site.baseurl}}/data/documents/woodworking/2026-05-storage/20260519_094318.jpg)

![Let's go with spray paint]({{site.baseurl}}/data/documents/woodworking/2026-05-storage/20260519_094325.jpg)

You actually got a decent coverage with spray paint. It leaves the grain of the wood visible a lot more than a brush paint would.

![spray paint coverage]({{site.baseurl}}/data/documents/woodworking/2026-05-storage/20260519_171511.jpg)

Maybe I just suck. But whatever I do, I seem to leave a seem between the passes with the spray paint.

![spray paint streaks]({{site.baseurl}}/data/documents/woodworking/2026-05-storage/20260519_173819.jpg)

With more tight passes they kinda go away. But the frustrating thing is that it doesn't seem to show as you spray, but rather as it dries.

![spray paint still streaks]({{site.baseurl}}/data/documents/woodworking/2026-05-storage/20260519_181438.jpg)

Once tey are inside, the indoor light pretty much hides the streaks,
so it's probably fine. And it's gonna go in a closet anyway. So I
shouldn't stress too much about it.

![with inside light]({{site.baseurl}}/data/documents/woodworking/2026-05-storage/20260520_093352.jpg)

In the closet. Yeah! looks good!
