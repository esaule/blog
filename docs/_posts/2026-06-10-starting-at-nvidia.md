After 13 years at UNC Charlotte, it was time for me to leave.  No
regret working there. But it was time for me to do something else.

If you were one of my students (in classes, or in research, or
whatever). I love you! Go do great in the world! Go learn more stuff!
If you need anything, reach out!

If you were a colleague, thanks for still working on helping students!
If I can help, let be know!

For myself, I'll be working for NVIDIA. I'll be working on
[NCCL](https://github.com/NVIDIA/nccl); it's a GPU communication
library. Think MPI type of things, but for GPUs. It's much more than
that. But you get the jist!
