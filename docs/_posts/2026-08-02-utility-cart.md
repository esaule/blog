Oh no! What did I do!?

My wife liked the [office storage unit]({%
post_url2026-05-20-office-storage %})). And now she wants me to build
stuff. That's how hell starts, isn't it?

She needs utility cart to put streaming gear and to put stuff on
display. (She does whatnot these days.). So they have to store stuff
and be mobile.

The plan is going to be easy: trays mounted on a frame. But since I'm
gonna glue the whole thing for stability, the trays can be part of the
frame.

1. side are plywood frame (like picture frame really)
2. trays are plywood trays. (probaly a better plywood)
3. wheels go on a mount glued under

The sides are just plywood frames.

![Sides]({{site.baseurl}}/data/documents/woodworking/2026-08-utilitycart/20260802_115759.jpg)

Forgot to snap a pic of the trays. Glueing them on turned out to be a painbecasue there are lots of clamping point. And you need to glue it all at once because it kinda of bows.

![You never have enough clamps now do you?]({{site.baseurl}}/data/documents/woodworking/2026-08-utilitycart/20260802_115906.jpg)

And done! I was afraid it is would rock. But just a little. Once
loaded, it's pretty sturdy. I wouldn't climb it. But to push light
stuff on it, it's fine!
