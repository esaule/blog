The idea is pretty simple.
Build a light shade where each side of it is a different recursive depth of a Hilbert curve.

It's a nice idea cause the Hilbert curve should cover the plane densely
so you should still get a fair amount of light through.

Prototyped it with a pine panel. With the plan of cutting the hilbert
curve with a router. And here is what I got..

![Yeah, it's ugly]({{site.baseurl}}/data/documents/woodworking/2025-07-light/IMG_20250706_135508927.jpg )


Yeah it's ugly... I learned a few things that day:

1. Pine is the wrong wood to do this. It's too brittle.
2. I suck at routing.
3. That what we invested laser cutters for! Tried the one on campus
   and that's kinda nice. Will do a light later, I guess.
