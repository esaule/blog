You remember the garage shelving I made a while back? [That one]({% post_url 2020-10-01-moregarageproject %}).

So it turns out, garages are not flat. So when you build on them, you build not straight. Over the years this has been tilting forward, and forward, and FORWARD. So we building a new one.

This one is gonna go on the wall. The plan is simple:

1. French cleat on the walls.
2. 2x4 mounted for wall mount at arbitrary height on the cleats and dimensioned from the ground to avoid any tilt.
3. cabinet based screwd on the wall mounts
4. more 2x4 for off the wall support
5. OSB for surface
